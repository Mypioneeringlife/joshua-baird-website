THE BACK ROOM

Ideas do not need permission.

I like projects that cross categories. A story can become a video. A practical problem can become a system, a tool, or a better way of doing something. A painting can become the visual language for something else. The useful question is not “what box does this fit in?” It is “is it worth building?”
